## Use Cases

### Launch a Single
1. Enter genre in Radio Autopilot → discover 6+ station types.
2. Select high-match targets → dispatch personalized pitches from rotating owner emails.
3. Switch to Press Wire → load tiered media outlets → distribute journalistic release.
4. Track opens and replies in the local email log.

### Book a Regional Tour
1. Enter state in Booking Agent → load real venues with contacts.
2. Send EPK + contract draft to talent buyers.
3. Engage Master Autopilot → detect routing gaps → find fill-in shows.
4. Trigger Town Takeover → local press + radio saturation.

### Maintain Monthly Momentum
1. Run Radio Autopilot weekly for new genre-specific stations.
2. Refresh Press Wire with new angles (tour, single, video).
3. Let the Booking Agent's persistence engine follow up until booked.

### Objection Handling

**"Why not just use a publicist?"**
> A publicist is great, but costs $1,000–$5,000/month. SoundScape Pilot handles the research, first-touch outreach, and follow-up scheduling for a one-time fee. You can still hire a publicist later for high-level strategy.

**"Is this legal?"**
> Yes. SoundScape Pilot uses public directories and APIs, respects robots.txt, and rotates sender identities to comply with anti-spam best practices. Users are responsible for following CAN-SPAM and platform ToS.

**"I'm not technical."**
> The Windows installer sets everything up. We also offer a Done-For-You Setup package that configures SMTP, DNS, and DKIM for you.

**"Will emails really land in inboxes?"**
> SoundScape Pilot rotates through four owner accounts and DKIM-signs messages. When paired with a warmed-up domain and proper SMTP, deliverability rivals professional email service providers.
