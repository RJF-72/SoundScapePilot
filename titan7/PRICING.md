## One-Time Purchase Fee

SoundScape Pilot is positioned as a **premium, self-hosted music industry command center**. It is sold as a **perpetual, one-time license** — not a subscription.

This price reflects the proprietary nature of the stack (real scrapers, SMTP engine, rotating owner accounts, contract drafting, press wire, and tour autopilot) and the fact that the buyer owns the software outright with no recurring SaaS fees.
